## 1. What ClaudeConnector is
ClaudeConnector is a private administration tool for Discord servers that I administer. It manages a server's channels, roles, permissions, onboarding, AutoMod rules, invites and webhooks, and it can post, edit and pin messages. It is operated only by me, sometimes with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic). ClaudeConnector has no commands for members, does not respond to messages and only works on servers I have explicitly configured.
